# Meta ads (Instagram + Facebook): "Talk to Krishna"

One offer, one action: **log in and talk to Krishna. The first minute each day is free.**
Send every ad to: `https://kundlikaka.in/gita?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=krishna_call&utm_content=<ad name>`

---

## 1. Campaign set-up (do this in Ads Manager)

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Objective | **Traffic**, optimise for **Landing page views** for the first 5 days. When Meta Pixel is on the site, switch to Leads / Complete registration. |
| Budget | ₹500 a day total, 5 to 7 days, before judging anything. |
| Ad sets | 3 ad sets with the same ads, so you learn the audience: (A) broad, (B) Krishna and Gita interests, (C) Hindi-speaking only. |
| Location | India. Add the top cities later, from the data. |
| Age | 22 to 55. |
| Language | Hindi + English (Hinglish copy works for both). |
| Interests for ad set B | Bhagavad Gita, Krishna, ISKCON, Meditation, Spirituality, Hindu devotional music. Keep the audience wide (over 5 million). |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements. Reels and Stories carry most of the cheap clicks; always make the creative 9:16. |
| Schedule | Push spend from 8 pm to 12 am IST, when people are in bed worrying or scrolling. |
| Call to action button | **Learn more** or **Sign up**. (Do not use "Call now", it dials a phone number.) |
| Ads per ad set | 4 creatives (see section 2). Turn off the losers after 3 days, double the winner. |

Track on your side: how many people tap **Call Krishna**, how many log in, how many reach the free minute, how many top up. The site already records `krishna_call_start`.

## 2. The creatives (start with these 4)

### Ad 1: "Raat ke 2 baje" (Reel, 15 seconds). Best first test
**Idea:** a person lying awake with a phone, then the call screen with Krishna speaking.
**On-screen text and voice-over (Hinglish):**
- 0 to 3 s, text: *"Raat ke 2 baje. Neend nahi aa rahi."* Show a dark room, a face lit by a phone.
- 3 to 6 s, text: *"Kisse baat karein?"* Phone screen opens kundlikaka.in.
- 6 to 12 s: screen recording of the call. Krishna's real voice from the site: *"वत्स, शांत हो जाओ... मैं तुम्हारे साथ हूँ."* Captions on screen.
- 12 to 15 s, text: *"Krishna se baat karo. Pehla minute free."* End card with the logo and the button.

### Ad 2: "Arjun ko bhi confusion tha" (Reel, 20 seconds)
- 0 to 3 s, text: *"Arjun bhi jung ke beech mein ghabra gaya tha."* Image of Krishna and Arjuna on the chariot.
- 3 to 10 s, voice-over: *"Tab Krishna ne usse samjhaya. Aaj bhi jab mann bechain ho, tum Krishna se baat kar sakte ho."*
- 10 to 17 s: call screen recording.
- 17 to 20 s, text: *"Login karo. Pehla minute free."*

### Ad 3: "Gita padhne ka time nahi?" (static image or 3-image carousel)
Headline: **Gita padhne ka time nahi? Krishna se baat karo.**
Text on the image: *"Seedhi baat. Hindi mein. Pehla minute free."*

### Ad 4: Real product shot (Reel or story, 10 seconds)
Screen recording only: tap Call Krishna, log in, Krishna asks your name, speaks to you by name. Caption: *"Krishna ne mera naam poocha."* Real products shown honestly often beat polished ads.

## 5. Keep the ads accepted and honest

- Say it is an **AI voice inspired by the Gita**. Do not say it is the real Krishna or that Krishna "will talk to you".
- Use "jab mann bechain ho" (when your mind is restless), not "tum depressed ho" or "kya aap anxiety se pareshan hain?". Ads that point at a person's health or feelings get rejected.
- No promises: no "your problems will end", no future-telling, no money or marriage guarantees.
- Do not mention remedies, gemstones or paid pujas in this ad.
- The "first minute free every day" claim is true, so keep it in the text, and keep the price (₹10 a minute after) visible on the landing page.

---

## 6. Judge the results

After 5 days, keep what works:
1. Click cost: below ₹8 is good for this audience, below ₹5 is great (these are rough targets, not promises).
2. Landing view to login: if people reach the page and do not log in, fix the page, not the ad.
3. Login to first call: this is the test of the product.
4. First call to top-up: this decides how much you can spend per login.

Rule of thumb for the maximum you can spend to get one logged-in caller: the average first top-up you earn from a caller, multiplied by the share who top up, minus the cost of their free minute (about ₹2 to ₹4).
