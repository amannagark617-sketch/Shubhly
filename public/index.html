      Kundli kaise banti hai?

      Swiss Ephemeris se sidereal (Lahiri) positions nikalti hain, whole-sign houses ke saath. AI chart nahi banata, sirf use samjhata hai.

      Janm ka time nahi pata, to?

      Phir bhi kundli banegi. Chandra rashi aur nakshatra dikhenge, par lagna aur dasha nahi, kyunki woh sahi time par nirbhar hain.

      Rashifal kaise banta hai?

      Chandrama aaj aapki rashi se kaunse ghar mein hai, aaj ki tithi aur din ka swami dekhkar. Yeh gochar ki paramparik reeti hai, isliye har rashi ke liye roz naya padhne ko milta hai.

      Kya yeh free hai?

      Launch ke dauran kundli, panchang aur rashifal free hain. Koi sign-up nahi.

      Mera data kahan jaata hai?

      Naam aur janm details sirf aapke device par yaad rakhe jaate hain (agar aap chaho). Server unhe save nahi karta.
